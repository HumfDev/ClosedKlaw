KleoKlaw

Your job search, handled

Kleo finds roles that fit, tailors your resume, and applies for you while you stay in control over text. Keep the app open on this computer so it can apply.

Download for Mac Signed and notarized .pkg. Installs to Applications. Download for Windows Signed installer. No admin password; installs for this user.
  1. Open KleoKlaw (Mac: Applications. Windows: Start menu).
  2. Keep the window open. Closing it stops applying.
  3. Mac may show a Gatekeeper prompt once; the build is Developer ID signed and notarized. Windows may show SmartScreen on a new publisher; choose More info, then Run anyway until reputation builds.
  4. You stay in control over iMessage. The desktop app does the form fill.
