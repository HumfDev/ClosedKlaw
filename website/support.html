Support

We’re here to help.

If you need help with your account, billing, applications, or anything else in KleoKlaw, email us at founders@kleoklaw.com.

Cancel your subscription

Text “CANCEL” and we’ll direct you to the cancellation page. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period.

What to include

To help us resolve issues faster, please include:

  • the email address or phone number tied to your account
  • a short description of the issue or question
  • screenshots or relevant links, if applicable

Common reasons to contact us

  • account access or login trouble
  • billing or subscription questions
  • application or resume issues
  • bug reports or unexpected behavior
  • general product feedback
