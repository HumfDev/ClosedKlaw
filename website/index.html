Frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions we hear most.

Does Kleo apply without asking me?

No, not by default. Every application waits for your approval over iMessage. You can optionally enable auto-apply for roles that match your criteria, and turn it off at any time by text.

Which job sites does Kleo support?

Kleo supports a growing set of applicant tracking systems used by employers. Coverage varies by employer and some applications require you to finish a step manually.

What data does Kleo use?

Your resume, job preferences, and the answers you provide, plus your messages with Kleo. When you approve an application, that information is sent to the employer or their applicant tracking system. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How much does it cost?

$50 per month (USD), with a 7-day free trial. A payment method is required. After the trial, the subscription renews monthly until you cancel.

How do I cancel?

Email founders@kleoklaw.com to cancel. Your subscription remains active until the end of the current billing period.
