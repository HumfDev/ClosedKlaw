Privacy Policy

Last updated August 18, 2026

This Privacy Notice for Kleo Labs Inc. ("we," "us," or "our") describes how we collect, use, store, and share personal information when you use KleoKlaw, our SMS and iMessage job-application assistant ("Services"). KleoKlaw searches and scores jobs, may tailor a résumé, fills ATS forms, and submits applications on Greenhouse and Workday on your behalf.

This notice covers when you:

  • Visit kleoklaw.com or any website of ours that links here
  • Message with Kleo via iMessage (SendBlue)
  • Upload a résumé, complete onboarding, pay, or use application automation
  • Install the local desktop agent
  • Join our waitlist or otherwise contact us

US audience. KleoKlaw is intended for users in the United States. Work-authorization questions, EEO-1 / self-identification forms, and iMessage delivery assume a US context. We do not actively market to residents of the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or Switzerland. If you access the Services from outside the United States, you do so at your own initiative. Information may be processed in the United States and in other countries where our processors operate, including Google Cloud regions and large-language-model providers that may process data outside the US (our primary model provider, DeepSeek, is based in the People's Republic of China).

California Notice at Collection. If you are a California resident, read Section 16 before you send a résumé or other personal information. By sending your résumé you agree to this Privacy Notice and our Terms of Service.

Questions? Contact founders@kleoklaw.com or Kleo Labs Inc., 15633 NE 173rd St, Woodinville, WA 98072, United States. If you do not agree with these practices, do not use the Services.

SUMMARY OF KEY POINTS

What we collect. Identity and two emails (job-application email and Google-verified account email), phone, address, résumé files and tailored PDFs, work authorization, education and experience, optional EEO / self-ID answers, job preferences, Workday passwords in a vault, iMessage threads, application snapshots, billing identifiers, and local-agent tokens. We do not collect Social Security numbers as a profile field. We do not collect biometrics or GPS.

Sensitive information. Work authorization, visa/sponsorship, citizenship, and optional EEO data (gender, Hispanic/Latino, race/ethnicity, veteran status, disability) are stored in plaintext on your user profile, not in the credential vault. Skip and "prefer not to answer" are different stored choices. We use EEO data only to fill employer self-ID forms you authorized. We do not use EEO data for job matching or scoring.

We do not sell personal information for money. We do share it constantly with employers when we apply for you, and with the processors listed in Sections 4 and 17. We do not share résumé, EEO, or application data for cross-context behavioral advertising. The marketing website uses Google Analytics.

Security is not bank-grade and not end-to-end. We generate, store, reset, and may text Workday passwords. Application-queue snapshots are plaintext in Postgres. SMS/iMessage is not end-to-end encrypted from a product perspective.

Your rights. You can view a 15-minute signed profile link, correct information over iMessage, and request deletion by emailing founders@kleoklaw.com. We do not currently offer an automated JSON export. STOP, cancel billing, and delete account are three different actions (see the Terms).

1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?

In Short: We collect the categories below. This list is meant to be complete for current product behavior, not illustrative.

Identity and contact

  • Legal first name, last name, and display name

Work authorization (sensitive)

EEO / protected-class self-identification (highly sensitive)

Stored in plaintext on the user profile, not in the credential vault:

  • Gender and pronouns
  • Hispanic/Latino
  • Race/ethnicity (EEO-1 categories)

Credentials and secrets (vault)

We do not collect Social Security numbers as a standing field. If a form requires SSN or government ID, we text a gap question rather than invent an answer.

Communications

  • 15-minute signed profile-view links (capability URLs; treat them like a password)

Application operational data

  • Frozen full-PII snapshots on each application-queue row

  • Browser-automation artifacts, which may include screenshots of name, address, and EEO fields
  • Per-tenant plus-addressed emails (for example user+tenant@gmail.com)
  • Desktop-agent auth token, claimed orders, and that the local machine is used to type passwords and click submit

Billing

  • Stripe customer id, subscription status, trial dates
  • Last4 / brand via Stripe (not the full card number / PAN)

Waitlist

  • Name, optional gender, job types, age attestation, Terms/Privacy version accepted, and timestamp

Website, cookies, and device

  • IP address, browser and device characteristics, pages viewed, referring URLs, approximate geography from IP (not GPS)
  • Google Analytics on kleoklaw.com (device identifiers, pages, approximate geography)
  • Cookies and similar storage for Google Sign-In, Stripe Checkout, sessions, and analytics

We do not currently honor Do Not Track signals (Section 11). We do not use a cookie consent banner because the site is intended for a US audience.

Sources

  • Résumé parser (LLM)
  • Employer ATS pages (options and required fields)
  • Job boards / ingest partners (job data, not your identity)

5. ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE AND AUTOMATED DECISIONS

We use large language models and automated scoring to extract résumés, tailor documents, parse SMS, rank jobs, and decide whether a match is strong enough to enqueue (at or above your minimum score). Auto-apply without per-job human review is possible if you turn auto-apply on and manual review off. A non-exhaustive large-employer list still pauses those applications for review. You can disable auto-apply, keep manual review on, skip EEO questions, disable Workday, or delete the account.

We do not warrant that tailored résumés or automated applications are undetectable. We do not warrant that matching will include every suitable job.

To limit automated application processing: disable auto-apply over iMessage, decline pending confirmations, or close the account. Replying STOP opts you out of proactive messages; it does not by itself disable auto-apply or cancel Stripe.

6. GOOGLE SIGN-IN, GMAIL, AND IMAP

If you register with Google Sign-In, we receive your name, email (account_email), and related profile fields from Google. Billing uses that account email, not necessarily the job-application email on your profile.

If you forward verification emails or grant IMAP access, we use it to retrieve ATS one-time codes and activation or reset links for applications you authorized. IMAP on a dedicated inbox is full mailbox access to that dedicated account, not your unrelated personal mail. We do not use Gmail or mailbox contents for advertising. If we access Google user data via Google APIs, we will use it only to provide or improve user-facing features of the Service consistent with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements, and not for serving ads.

You can revoke Google access in your Google account and/or by contacting us. Revoking access may prevent us from completing applications that need a verification code.

7. HOW LONG DO WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION?

These periods reflect current compaction and deletion code. They are operational intent, not a guarantee that every replica is gone on that day. Counsel has not separately certified them.

  • Application-queue terminal rows (submitted, permanent failure, cancelled, tailoring failed, expired): about 365 days, unless you delete the account sooner
  • Notification-queue consumed rows: about 24 hours
  • Inbound SMS ledger: about 7 days
  • Job pool (raw jobs): about 60 days
  • Stale matches / completed match-compute jobs: about 7 days
  • Events / Stripe events: about 90 days
  • Conversation (agent_messages): about 180 days
  • Agent memory: until account deletion (no nightly compact rule)
  • Vault audit: about 730 days
  • Vault credentials (Workday passwords, IMAP app passwords): until account deletion. Turning Workday off skips new Workday auto-applies; it does not purge the vault
  • Résumés and artifacts in R2: until account deletion (no nightly TTL). Do not assume every historical copy was always deleted on schedule
  • Stripe: according to Stripe and tax law (often years)
  • Waitlist: until conversion or up to 24 months
  • Backups, Cloud Run logs, /tmp debug snapshots, SendBlue history, LLM-provider logs, shared IMAP copies: may outlast the periods above. Local worker snapshots are typically short-lived and not guaranteed to survive restarts. Browser-automation profiles on machines used for submit may survive account deletion

Account deletion

Email founders@kleoklaw.com to request deletion. From active systems we attempt to remove: user profile, preferences, settings, vault entries (DEK zeroized), matches, pending actions, agent memory, onboarding session, agent/recruiter messages, Postgres queue rows for that user (including submitted-status rows), and R2 objects under users/{user_id}/. Postgres and R2 purge is best-effort after the vault key is zeroized; a failure is logged as incomplete.

What remains: applications already sitting at employers (we cannot un-file them); Stripe customer records unless cancelled separately; SendBlue message history; LLM provider logs; shared-inbox copies of forwarded mail; backups until they expire; logs and screenshots as described above. We do not currently keep a separate "already applied" ledger after deletion in our application queue — those rows are deleted with the account — but employers still have what we submitted.

8. HOW DO WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION SAFE?

We use a per-user data-encryption key for vault secrets, wrapped with a system key held in Secret Manager / environment configuration. Equal-opportunity and other demographic answers are not in that vault. Application-queue payloads are plaintext PII snapshots in Postgres. iMessage is not end-to-end encrypted from a product perspective. We generate, store, reset, and SMS Workday passwords; we do not claim we never see them. The local agent receives Workday passwords.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Transmission of personal information to and from the Services is at your own risk.

Incidents. If we discover a breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you and regulators as required by applicable law. Many US states require notice without unreasonable delay; some clocks are 30–60 days, and some are faster.

Withdrawing consent. You may withdraw consent by changing settings, skipping EEO, opting out of Workday, STOP, or deletion. Withdrawal does not undo processing already done or applications already submitted.
